## KEY STATISTICS HIGHLIGHTED

### Market Size
- **Current:** $197B SaaS market (2023) | **Projected:** $299B by 2026 (18.4% CAGR)
- **Data Pipeline Market:** $12.09B (2024) → $48.33B (2030)
- **Embedded Analytics:** $23.41B (2025) → $100.98B (2035)
- **Conversational AI:** $41.39B by 2030 (23.7% CAGR)

### Competitive Opportunities
- **92%** of SaaS companies have AI on roadmap
- **60%** of web traffic is mobile (mobile-first design is standard)
- **60%** of dashboards are still static (AI = differentiation opportunity)
- **40%** of dashboards already have AI features (market moving fast)
- **47%** of websites fail Core Web Vitals assessment (big UX/SEO opportunity)
- **89%** of SaaS board decks require NRR visibility
- **6 of 8** top KPI tools now have native Stripe/HubSpot/Salesforce integrations

### SaaS Metrics Benchmarks
- **MRR Growth Target:** 25% month-over-month (early stage)
- **Churn Target:** <5% monthly for SMB SaaS
- **NRR Target:** >110% (net revenue retention)
- **CAC Payback:** <12 months
- **Rule of 40:** Growth% + LTV:CAC ratio = 40+
- **Enterprise ACV:** $50K+ (typical for SaaS Revenue Ops segment)

### Compliance Deadlines
- **WCAG 2.1 AA:** April 26, 2027 (larger public entities), May 11, 2027 (healthcare)
- **SOC 2 Type II:** Timeline = 12-18 months from start (3-12 month control operation + audit)
- **GDPR Penalties:** Up to €20M or 4% of global revenue
- **Core Web Vitals:** Still a Google ranking factor; 100ms delay = 7% conversion drop
